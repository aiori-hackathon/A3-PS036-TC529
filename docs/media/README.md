# Media assets

The logo files in this directory, except `source/`, are generated by
`scripts/logo/make_assets.py` from the source artwork in
`source/climb-logo-original.png`. The PNGs are build outputs: re-run the
script instead of editing them by hand, and let the script refresh the table
below so the sizes stay true. The commit-graph artifacts are separate; they
are described at the end of this file.

## How they were produced

The source artwork is a 1408 × 768 PNG on a white panel with a pale margin.
The script turns it into the published assets in five steps.

1. **Find the content.** The ink bounding box is measured from the source
   (x 470–937, y 98–682) and cropped to with a 4 % margin. The crop also
   drops the decorative sparkle in the lower-right corner of the panel,
   which sits outside the bounding box.
2. **Rebuild the transparency.** The paper and the panel margin become
   transparent. The ink keeps its own colour: the colour of an edge pixel is
   recovered by projecting it onto the measured colour of its family, so an
   anti-aliased pixel keeps the colour of the shape it belongs to instead of
   fading towards white. The anti-aliasing itself moves into the alpha
   channel, so no white fringe survives on either background.
3. **Recolour for dark backgrounds.** On the dark variants the slate ink
   becomes `#E6EDF3`. The orange and teal inks already clear 3:1 against
   `#0D1117` and are kept as they are; the measured ratios are in
   `brand.toml`.
4. **Cut the variants.** The tagline is dropped for the marks, the crest is
   cropped out of the master and padded into a square canvas for the icon,
   and every icon derivative is resized from that same square canvas, so the
   small sizes are the same drawing and not a separate one. The mark ends six
   clear rows below the wordmark's last visible pixel, so none of the
   tagline's faint upper anti-aliasing survives into it.
5. **Optimise.** The PNGs are written with the maximum deflate level;
   `oxipng` and `pngquant` are used in their place when they are installed.

`python3 scripts/logo/make_assets.py --check` re-reads the committed marks and
fails if the bottom six rows contain any non-transparent pixel, or if the image
no longer ends exactly six rows after its last visible ink. A normal rebuild
runs the same check on the new marks before writing them.

The vector rendition of the crest is optional: it needs a tracer on the
path, and the raster icon is the published fallback. When the tracer is
missing the script says so and writes no SVG.

## The palette

`brand.toml` holds the sampled palette: the paper and margin colours, the
ink colour of each family, how many pixels of that family the artwork
carries, the contrast of each ink against the light and the dark background,
and the colour decision taken for the dark variants.

## The commit graph

`cargo xtask commit-graph` writes five files here from the repository's public
history: `commit-graph.json` (the document), `commit-graph.svg` (the wide card,
840 × 540), `commit-graph-narrow.svg` (the phone card, 400 × 640),
`commit-graph-compact.svg` (the wide strip, 840 × 156) and
`commit-graph-compact-narrow.svg` (the phone strip, 400 × 200). The cards are
rendered from the document and repeat its headline numbers in their `<desc>`
element. The README shows the compact strip under the badges and the full card
under "Built in the open", one variant per viewport. Re-run the command instead
of editing any of the five by hand. `docs/MEDIA.md` describes the
configuration, the layout and the checks that keep the artifacts honest.
